Arm the bomb in the existing bow slot with key 2. Hold Attack and aim to see a faint dashed lob and an orange blast ellipse. The preview is visible only to the thrower. With a mouse, the landing follows the cursor up to the maximum throw range from the hero's aim origin. On touch, drag from the Attack button to set direction and distance. A 120-screen-pixel drag from its center reaches full range, and shorter drags scale proportionally. Drag back inside the 8-viewport-pixel dead zone to cancel. Sword mode still shows the swing cone. Interact mode does not aim a throw.

Tap Attack without dragging past the dead zone, or press Space without an active aim, to throw at the nearest living enemy player, mob or enemy soldier in range. If no enemy is in range, the pot lands at 60% of the maximum range in the hero's facing direction.

The host validates the throw, caps its reach and decides when the pot lands and explodes. The pot flies over terrain, lands with a lit fuse, and bursts. While the fuse burns, every peer draws a dashed red-orange warning ellipse at the landing, the same flattened size as the blast. The warning is not part of the thrower's aim preview. The blast tests player and mob positions and tile centers against an ellipse with a vertical radius of 60% of its horizontal radius. It skips the thrower and their soldiers. Free-form palisade cells take one existing wall hit per damage point.

The Bomb category in Tuning defaults to 160 px throw range, 40 px horizontal blast radius, 1.0 s fuse, 1.5 s cooldown, and 2 damage. A throw during the cooldown is ignored. The Attack button uses its existing disabled art while the bomb is recharging. The tool-wheel bow slot uses the clay pot icon on the sage disc (normal, pressed, selected, selected-pressed, and disabled), generated by `tools/ui/gen_hud_e4.py`.

- **Host seam.** `test_bomb_shot` checks a mid-range throw, blast geometry, flight over a wall, validation, cooldown, fuse timing, and damage to actors and palisades. About 1.5 s.
- **Auto-target seam.** `test_bomb_auto_target` checks nearest in-range enemies, skips an allied soldier and a dead player, and checks the facing fallback. Under 1 s.
- **Cooldown seam.** `test_bomb_cooldown` rejects a second throw during the wait, accepts one after it, and checks that a longer `bomb.cooldown_seconds` value lengthens the wait. Under 2 s.
- **Two peers.** `test_bomb_two_players` uses seed 11 and oversize requests in both directions to check the host range cap. Both replicas see the host's three phases, the fuse warning ellipse at the same 40 px radius, and agree on enemy health and unchanged thrower health. About 5–10 s per stress run.
- **Live drive.** Run `bin/launch.sh drives/ranged_bow.gd --display`. Expect `bomb-preview-short`, `bomb-preview-medium`, and `bomb-preview-long` to show the same blast area at increasing distances along a faint dashed trail. Expect `bomb-hud-icon` and `bomb-wheel-open` to show the clay pot on the tool disc and in the open wheel. Expect `bomb-cooldown-rejected` after a second throw during the wait (no second pot) and `bomb-cooldown-ready` after a throw once the cooldown has elapsed. Expect `bomb-mid-arc` with an unlit pot, `bomb-landed-fuse` with the dashed red-orange warning ellipse, and `bomb-explosion` at the long preview's landing point, and `bomb-tap-auto-target` with a lit pot near the enemy.
