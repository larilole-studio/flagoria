# Stone wall fronts

Drive: drives/stone_wall_fronts.gd
Source: Scripts/mechanics/walls/wall_solver.gd

Stone and moss walls show a five-pixel-deep horizontal cap and a
six-pixel-wide vertical cap with a shaded side. The front spans two stone
courses. Both stone archer-post variants share their joining edges with the
corresponding run.

Host a game to see the starting castle walls. The drive hosts with seed 11
and captures the castle from the spawn. Compare the north horizontal run,
both vertical runs and the four corners in the `wall-runs-and-corners` frame.
