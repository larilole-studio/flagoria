# Clay pot bomb throw

Drive `ranged_bow.gd` on seed 49, after arming the bow tool.

1. Aim cone before release.
2. Clay pot at the top of its arc, clearing the keep.
3. Landed pot showing the burning-fuse frame.
4. Explosion burst at the landing.
5. Short drag: orange blast ellipse close to the hero, faint dashed trail.
6. Medium drag: the same ellipse farther along the throw.
7. Long drag: the ellipse at max range, still flattened to the map perspective.
8. Tap with no drag: the pot auto-targets the nearest enemy (the boar) and lands beside it.
9. The tool disc shows the clay pot icon, not the bow.
10. The open tool wheel shows the clay pot on the bow slot, with sword and chop unchanged.
11. A second throw during the cooldown is ignored; the Attack button uses its disabled art, and only the first pot is on the ground.
12. After the cooldown, a throw lands again.
13. After the pot lands, a dashed red-orange warning ellipse (same flattened size as the blast) is drawn at the landing until it explodes.
14. After archer mode is removed, the open tool wheel shows the clay pot between sword and chop. There is no bow icon or Bow label.

Crops are the same frames, tighter on the throw, the landing, or the HUD.
